WordSnap visual directions

Five ways this product could feel.

These are not recorder/player mockups. They borrow the references' restraint, mono type, tactile spacing, dark/light contrast, and red/orange signal language, then turn that into five different web and device UX systems.

The most Anki-centered direction. Instead of showing a production line, it shows where each word lives: inbox, card, library. Best if the emotional reward is seeing the library grow.
